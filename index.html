How big is that animal really?

An animal stands next to you on graph paper. Resize it to how big you think it really is.

A big animal sits on one side of a scale. Pour smaller animals onto the other side until they balance. Weights stay hidden until you lock in.

A log lies across a rock like a seesaw. Drop animals onto it and keep it level — weight far from the rock counts for far more than weight near it.

  1. 1Enter your height. That is your silhouette, drawn to scale.
  2. 2Drag the red corner on the animal until it looks life-size beside you.
  3. 3Lock it in. Closer guesses score more, out of 100. Five animals, 500 points.
  1. 1Click the bucket to drop one animal, and hold to tip it and pour faster. Right-drag the bucket — or drag it with a finger — to carry it anywhere on the paper and pour there. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
  2. 2Nothing comes back out, so ease off before you overshoot. The scale bobs, but it will not tell you the answer.
  3. 3Lock it in to see both weights and the true multiple. Five weigh-ins, 500 points.
  1. 1Click anywhere above the log to drop the animal waiting in the dock. It falls, bounces once and settles where it lands.
  2. 2Turning force is weight × distance from the rock, so the heavy ones belong close in and the light ones far out. Every animal carries its weight at its centre of gravity.
  3. 3Ten animals a load. Tip too far and they slide off and are lost. Ten points for every one still aboard, so 500 across five loads.

Credits & sources

Silhouettes are from PhyloPic, a free database of organism silhouettes. Most are dedicated to the public domain (CC0); the ones marked CC BY require this credit.

    Body sizes and weights are typical adult figures from Wikipedia species pages, checked in September 2026, plus breed references for sheep and pigs. Where a source quotes shoulder height, the silhouette's own proportions convert it to the height of its highest point, which is what the size game scores. Weights match the adult each silhouette depicts, for example a male lion and a bull elephant.
